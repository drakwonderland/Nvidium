About Nvidium

How a dorm-room rig rescue effort along Katipunan became a Quezon City workstation workshop and local IT partner.

Origin Story

Nvidium started inside a shared Loyola Heights apartment after architecture and engineering students kept losing thesis renders to overheated mall desktops and undersized power supplies. Word spread from UP Diliman to nearby 5-to-25 person studios and clinics facing ₱45,000/month corporate IT minimums.

Named for the graphics and compute silicon behind modern CAD and 3D work, Nvidium now runs a 28 sqm static-safe workshop on Esteban Abada—ordering fresh distributor parts on a 70% down payment, soak-testing every rig for 24 hours, and delivering 4-hour local office care.

Mission

Build 24-hour stress-tested workstations with transparent part pricing, and keep neighborhood studios and offices running with fast 4-hour IT support.

Vision

Grow into the trusted custom GPU workstation builder and small-office IT partner across Quezon City’s university corridor within three years.

Goals, Permits & Team

Year 1: Break even

Deliver 9 PCs/month and sign 6 monthly office support clients to cover ₱94,500 in monthly expenses with minimal warranty returns.

Year 2: Repay startup cost

Earn back the ₱345,000 startup budget by Month 12, grow to 12 monthly support clients, and add a second technician once builds pass 14/month.

Year 3: School lab orders

Upgrade to 12% VAT registration above ₱3.0M annual sales and supply multi-PC school computer labs.
